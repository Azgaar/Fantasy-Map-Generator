# Azgaar's Fantasy Map Generator v1.65.2: New Heightmap Template: Taklamakan

Here is a very small update: heightmap templates are refactor and a new one is added - Taklamakan. The template generates a valley in the map center surrounded by high mountains. Due to the mountains the value is lacking precipitation and usually forms big desert.

The landscape generated resembles me the [Taklamakan desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taklamakan_Desert) in Asia. That's one of my favorite desert and I decided to stick with this name for the template. I do realize most people don't know that Taklamakan is, so let's consider this as educational effort. The desert is so interesting that I bet you will enjoy checking it on [Goggle maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/38%C2%B054'00.0%22N+82%C2%B012'00.0%22E/@38.29777,81.1607731,2386156m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0x0!8m2!3d38.9!4d82.2?hl=en).
