# Danish glossary

Terms every `da.json` string uses the same way. Rules for all catalogs are in
[docs/architecture/translation.md](../../../docs/architecture/translation.md).

## Style

- Address the user with **du**: “Klik for at redigere bebyggelsen”, “Er du sikker på, at du vil slette …?”.
- Buttons and menu items take the imperative or a noun: “Gem kort”, “Slet alle”.
- Tooltips are a short imperative or a noun phrase.
- Quotes are “ ”. Keys keep Latin names: Ctrl, Shift, Alt, Enter, Esc.
- Brand and product names stay: Azgaar, Armoria, Dropbox, Discord.
- The product names are translated: “Fantasykortgenerator”, in full “Azgaars Fantasykortgenerator”; the assistant is “Azgaar-assistenten”.
