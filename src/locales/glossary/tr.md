# Turkish glossary

Terms every `tr.json` string uses the same way. Rules for all catalogs are in
[docs/architecture/translation.md](../../../docs/architecture/translation.md).

## Style

- Address the user with **sen** only implicitly (verb forms); avoid pronouns: “Yerleşimi düzenlemek için tıkla”, “… silmek istediğine emin misin?”.
- Buttons and menu items take the bare noun or verb stem: “Haritayı kaydet”, “Tümünü kaldır”.
- Tooltips are a short imperative (“tıkla”) or a noun phrase.
- Quotes are “…”. Keys keep Latin names: Ctrl, Shift, Alt, Enter, Esc, Space.
- Brand and product names stay: Azgaar, Armoria, Dropbox, Discord.
- The product names are translated: “Fantezi Harita Oluşturucu”, in full “Azgaar'ın Fantezi Harita Oluşturucusu”; the assistant is “Azgaar Asistanı”.
- Turkish is agglutinative: a placeholder inside a sentence gets no suffix; reword so none is needed.
