# Romanian glossary

Terms every `ro.json` string uses the same way. Rules for all catalogs are in
[docs/architecture/translation.md](../../../docs/architecture/translation.md).

## Style

- Address the user with **tu**: “Apasă pentru a edita așezarea”, “Sigur vrei să elimini harta?”.
- Buttons and menu items take the imperative: “Salvează harta”, “Șterge tot”.
- Tooltips are a short imperative or a noun phrase.
- Quotes are „ ” in Romanian text; keys keep Latin names: Ctrl, Shift, Alt, Enter, Esc.
- Brand and product names stay: Azgaar, Armoria, Dropbox, Discord, GitHub.
- The product names are translated: “Generatorul de hărți fantasy”, in full “Generatorul de hărți fantasy al lui Azgaar”; the assistant is “Asistentul Azgaar”.
- “per” becomes “pe” (“pe piață”, “pe stat”).

## Tight labels

Where a label sits in a narrow box (layer list, bottom bar, unit fields), the shorter form is used
everywhere the key appears: “Harta înălțimi” (heightmap layer and tool), “Rozetă” (wind rose), “Mărime
hartă”, “Unitate distanță”, “Zoom −” (zoom out button), “Scurtături” (hotkeys).
