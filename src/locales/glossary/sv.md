# Swedish glossary

Terms every `sv.json` string uses the same way. Rules for all catalogs are in
[docs/architecture/translation.md](../../../docs/architecture/translation.md).

## Style

- Address the user with **du**: “Klicka för att redigera bosättningen”, “Är du säker på att du vill ta bort …?”.
- Buttons and menu items take the imperative or a noun: “Spara karta”, “Ta bort alla”.
- Tooltips are a short imperative or a noun phrase.
- Quotes are “ ”. Keys keep Latin names: Ctrl, Shift, Alt, Enter, Esc.
- Brand and product names stay: Azgaar, Armoria, Dropbox, Discord.
- The product names are translated: “Fantasykartgenerator”, in full “Azgaars Fantasykartgenerator”; the assistant is “Azgaar-assistenten”.
