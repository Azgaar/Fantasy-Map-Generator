# Bulgarian glossary

Terms every `bg.json` string uses the same way. Rules for all catalogs are in
[docs/architecture/translation.md](../../../docs/architecture/translation.md).

## Style

- Address the user formally with the plural **вие**: “Щракнете, за да промените селището”, “Сигурни ли сте, че искате да изтриете?”.
- Buttons and menu items are nouns: “Запазване”, “Отказ”, “Изтриване на всичко”. The bottom-row zoom-out button is the imperative “Смали”: a noun makes the row wrap.
- Tooltips are a short imperative (“Щракнете, за да …”, “Въведете …”) or a noun phrase.
- Capitalize only the first word of a label: “Добавяне на етикет”, “Показване на всички слоеве”.
- Quotes are “ ”. Keys keep their English names: Ctrl, Shift, Alt, Enter, Esc, Space.
- Brand and product names stay: Azgaar, Armoria, Dropbox, Discord, Google Fonts.
- The product names are translated: “Генератор на фантастични карти”, in full “Генераторът на фантастични карти на Azgaar”; the assistant is “Асистент Azgaar”.
- Stored ids the code compares literally stay English in quotes: the area unit “square”.
